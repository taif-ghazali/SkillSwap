## PHASE E: CRITICAL BUSINESS RULES

Implement and verify against the actual backend:

1. New users receive exactly 2 credits.
2. Booking requires at least 1 available credit.
3. Pending bookings must be accounted for.
4. Users cannot book their own slots.
5. Cancellation is permitted only at least 2 hours before the session.
6. Both tutor and learner must confirm.
7. Credits transfer exactly once after both confirmations.
8. One confirmation alone must not transfer credits.
9. Completed sessions can be rated.
10. Every credit transfer must have a ledger entry.

Credit modifications must remain server-side. Never implement client-side credit transfers.
