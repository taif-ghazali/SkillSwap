# SkillSwap UoH &mdash; Campus Privacy & Safety Policy
**School of Computer and Information Sciences (SCIS), University of Hyderabad**  
*IE209 Skill Development Mini-Project &bull; Course Requirement Section 8*  
*Effective Date: November 2026*

---

## 1. Core Principles
SkillSwap is designed exclusively for verified students and scholars of the University of Hyderabad to exchange academic skills, technical knowledge, and research techniques through **Time Credits (1 hour = 1 credit)**.

SkillSwap is strictly a peer-learning network &mdash; **no money, no commercial transactions, and no paid tutoring** are permitted under any circumstances.

---

## 2. Campus Safety & Meeting Venue Rules
To ensure the physical safety and comfort of all participants:

1. **Mandatory Public Campus Places**:
   - In-person peer learning sessions must take place **exclusively in public university facilities**:
     - Indira Gandhi Memorial (IGM) Library (Ground floor discussion halls, reading rooms)
     - School of Computer & Information Sciences (SCIS) labs, foyer, and seminar rooms
     - School of Humanities & Social Sciences discussion spaces
     - Campus cafeteria / open amphitheatre
2. **Strict Prohibition on Private Rooms**:
   - Mentoring sessions in hostel rooms, off-campus private residences, or secluded areas are strictly forbidden.
3. **Approved Online Sessions**:
   - Remote sessions must use institutional video links (e.g. Google Meet with `@uohyd.ac.in` domain).

---

## 3. Academic Integrity & University Honor Code
The University of Hyderabad maintains strict standards of academic honesty:

1. **Learning and Concept Mastery Only**:
   - Mentoring sessions are intended for conceptual explanations, debugging guidance, software tutorials (LaTeX, Python, Figma, Excel, Git), and research methodology.
2. **Strict Prohibition on Graded Work**:
   - Tutors and learners are strictly prohibited from doing, solving, or writing another student’s assignments, homework, lab records, term papers, thesis drafts, quizzes, or exams.
3. **Zero Tolerance for Contract Cheating**:
   - Any attempt to use time credits to contract out coursework will lead to immediate suspension from SkillSwap and reporting to the SCIS Departmental Disciplinary Committee.

---

## 4. Privacy & Data Minimization
SkillSwap adheres to strict data minimization principles:

1. **No Phone Numbers or Private Identifiers**:
   - The platform never collects, stores, or exposes personal mobile numbers, Aadhaar numbers, or student bank details.
2. **Public Directory Visibility**:
   - Other campus students can only view:
     - Public campus nickname (e.g., *Ravi Teja*, *Sneha R.*)
     - University institutional email (`...@uohyd.ac.in`)
     - Listed skills offered to teach and skills wanted
     - Verified tutor rating and peer reviews
3. **Immutable Credit Ledger**:
   - Time credit transactions are immutably logged on the server ledger (`Ledger` sheet) recording only timestamp, sender email, recipient email, credits moved, and session slot ID.

---

## 5. Misconduct Reporting & Administrative Enforcement
1. **In-App Reporting**:
   - Every session card and modal includes a **"Report Violation"** action.
   - Violations are categorized under:
     - Academic integrity breaches
     - Inappropriate meeting venues
     - Cash / monetary solicitation
     - Harassment or unprofessional behavior
     - Habitual no-shows
2. **Investigation & Action**:
   - Incident reports are dispatched to the `Reports` spreadsheet tab for administrator and course coordinator review.
   - Administrators reserve the right to revoke user access, freeze time credit balances, and notify university authorities.
